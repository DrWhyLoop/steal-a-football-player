# Game overview
"Steal a Footballer" is a Roblox steal-and-collect game (like Steal a Brainrot) with original cartoon football characters. 6 plots per server, max 6 players. Studio is edited through the MCP; src/ is synced to Studio and mirrors it.

## Core loop
1. Players break CAGES (not eggs; code still says Cube/Egg in places) with a pickaxe in a 12-stage hallway. Stage 1 is nearest the bases. Hallway is 192 studs wide, 6,200 studs long (stages 1-8 about 442.86, stages 9-10 553.57, stage 11 664.29, stage 12 885.71; Config.StageLengths is the source of truth).
2. Every stage spawns nine regular cages, three each of three exclusive designs (36 designs total, Config.Cages.ByStage), at random safe positions with clearance (Config.EggSpawning). Quotas come from CubeSpawn markers; Stage 12 has 4. Respawn Config.CubeRespawnSeconds. First 5 cages of a profile are an onboarding trickle (Config.Onboarding: 3 hits, Stage 1).
3. At 0 HP the server rolls everything at once (footballer, luck, size, variant, income roll). Clients (RewardEffects) show the cage breaking into cosmetic fragments, a white character growing for 1.5-2.9 s according to luck, a Neon flash, then a smoke/ring evolution burst (Config.EvolutionVFX) as the real footballer appears. Pickup is enabled after the reveal transition. Total reveal 2.35-3.75 s.
4. A hatched footballer has 30 cumulative uncarried seconds (carry pauses, Drop resumes). The player carries it home (slower for higher tiers), crosses the safe line (secured: no timer, no Drop, converted to one native hotbar tool "pocket" item), then places it on one of 30 displays across three floors of their plot (open ramp access, no E).
5. Placed footballers accrue cash into the slot (Pending). The owner collects by stepping on the green pad; upgrades via the yellow stand button (ClickDetector or R prompt).
6. Stealing: other players can steal a placed footballer (original-owner validation, reserved slot BEING STOLEN, recovery on pickaxe/guard hit). Mesh Bat knocks a carrier down and makes them drop. Stage guards chase carriers.

## Tiers and stages
Common 1-2, Rare 3-4, Epic 5-7, Legendary 8-9, Mythic 10-11, GOAT 12. Tier income per second 1 / 9.3 / 82 / 730 / 6.5K / 57.7K. A timed Golden cage (stages 4-11) drops one tier higher (capped Mythic). Timed Stage 12 GOAT event cage and a Titanic event (every 5 h, random stage weighted to later stages, 100x HP, GOAT tier, Gold or better) exist. Stage names, difficulty and recommended Speed Points are in Config.StageInfo (shown by banner and entrance signs).

## Footballers, sizes, variants
- 14 roster players (Config.PlayerRoster, Player_01-Player_14, reference-textured rigs; Player_02 awaits a replacement model; no walk/idle set beyond default Roblox clips).
- Ten size tiers (0.6x-3x, Config.RewardSizes) and ten variants (Config.RewardVariantOrder/Variants) shared across the roster. Server luck (Config.Luck: normal 1.0-1.5, Lucky Cage 3-4) weights both rolls and the per-tier IncomeRoll (Config.IncomeRange). Raw size x variant x income roll counts fully to 6x, then 6 + sqrt(raw-6), absolute ceiling 20x (Shared/RewardMath). Cards keep SizeTier, Variant, IncomeRoll, RewardVersion through carry, placement, save and steal. Legacy cards default to Normal.
- Displays compress all ten sizes into the slot space; carry restores full height. Variant effects are client only (at most 8 nearest, 2 particles/s, fade 60-80 studs).
- Rebirth (Config.Rebirth, Server/RebirthService, Shared/Rebirth, Client/RebirthUI): the bar fills on a log scale of Speed Points against 8e10 x 1.25^(n-1); at full the player can rebirth up to 10 times. It resets ONLY Speed Points; cash, plot footballers, tools, trails, treadmills and upgrades are kept. Each rebirth adds +0.5 to the income multiplier (CashService.Boost) and +0.5 to the Speed gain multiplier (TreadmillService training). Profile field Rebirths. Simulated in tools/economy/rebirth.py.
- Quests (Config.Quests, Server/QuestService, Client/QuestUI): 3 of 5 daily quests (UTC day seed) and all 5 weekly (UTC week, Monday start) pay Quest Tokens (profile fields QuestTokens, Quests; player attribute QuestTokens; QuestService.AddTokens spends/gains). Tracked server-side only: Break (CubeService), Place and Steal (SlotService.occupy; a re-placed card is not a new Place; Steal needs a victim account older than 3 days, once per victim per day), Collect (pad), Play (a minute counts only if the player moved or trains). Collect targets scale with plot income. Tokens never for Robux (rule above).
- Upgrades shop (Config.Upgrades, Server/UpgradeShop, Client/UpgradeUI, docs/Plans.md section 3; opened from the nav dock's More menu): Income (10 lv, +2.5% each, tokens), Speed gain (10 lv, +5%, cash), Collect pad range (5 lv, +1.5 studs, tokens), Cage luck (5 lv, +0.1 luck, tokens; added to the roll, total clamped at Luck.LuckyMax). Profile field Upgrades {id=level}, mirrored as Player attributes Upgrade_<id>; kept on rebirth.
- Collection Index book, daily reward, rare-hatch announcements (Config.Index/Daily/Announce). Index variant bitmask order must only be appended.

## Economy and shops (targets from tools/economy sims, not live proof)
- Target: Stage 12 in about 3.5-3.9 hours for a normal player; Stage 11 arrival is the Speed cap (200 studs/s at about 31.9B Speed Points).
- Cash and Speed Points are Player attributes owned by Server/PlayerStats (doubles, caps 1e60, saved as "%.17g" strings). Walking speed is never shown; the HUD shows CASH and SPEED (points). NumberFormat.Compact: K/M/B/T/Qa/Qi/Sx/Sp/Oc/No/Dc, then scientific.
- Treadmills (Config.Treadmills): 16 designs, one owned per player, walk-on/walk-off training (+5 points per second per multiplier, SpeedCurve in log10), cash tiers plus Celestial (game pass). Premium Celestial 1,500x.
- Tools (Config.CashTools): 20 cash tools with rarity; premium tools (Hammer, Marrow Reaper, Drill, Gilded Auger, Robot Arm Drill, Laser as Coming soon) use the power rule: best owned cash tool power x multiplier (max 1.5x), capped by MaxHP/5 per hit. Every player holds one mining tool plus the Mesh Bat.
- Trails (Config.Trails): 11 cash trails multiply training gain only (x2..x25K). 2x Cash game pass (Config.CashPass) multiplies plot income.
- Robux: every purchase goes through Server/Purchases (+PurchaseLog). GamePassId is 0 until the owner creates passes; Studio test mode simulates. ProcessReceipt uses a single-save atomic pattern with an in-flight guard; no developer products exist.
- Premium tools and any future product must not touch luck or which footballer rolls without showing odds.

## Guards and world
- One monster per stage (12), 1.5x visual scale after a 12 stud minimum; patrol inside the stage; chase only a carrier of an unsecured card from their own stage, within Config.GuardDetectRadius (150). Hit = fall and recovery, drops the card. Each guard is one unanchored root driven by AlignPosition/AlignOrientation with welded visuals; Stage 6 is the passive Eye.
- Environment: 120 torches (night lights), weather cycle (Sunny/Rain/Evening/Night, WeatherService), jungle of about 2,286 trees, lakes, four treehouses with stone paths, animals, Football Court with a server-owned ball and a 4v4/11v11 lineup staging lobby (no scoring/settlement yet), 32 spectator NPCs, three plaza leaderboards. StreamingEnabled with radii 400/800 (set in Studio, not src). The base lock feature was removed (2026-11-11).

# Known issues and open decisions
- Release blockers: published-server save/rejoin/outage testing; steals are NOT an atomic cross-profile transaction (a crash between the victim and thief saves can duplicate or lose a card; mitigated by ItemId, leave-skip and ordered saves); no real DataStore verification (Studio is memory-only); real two-player multiplayer and phone/tablet/gamepad testing (taps, layout, performance) never done; premium pass IDs not created (GamePassId=0) and no real purchase tested.
- Studio viewport often renders blank when Studio is in the background; several visual results (burst, billboards, guard gaits) were verified numerically only. Capture tool does not draw AlwaysOnTop billboards.
- Economy and balance are simulation-only: variant stacking, tier income, trip times, upgrade costs, Celestial/2x Cash value and guard escape rates need real-player tuning. Guard foot slide on fast stages is not measured; LongArm/Teddy clips are analytic estimates.
- Football: lineup staging only; no scoring, settlement or player control. Actual two-client acceptance untested.
